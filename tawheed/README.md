# ﷲ Tawheed — The 99 Names of Allah (Asma-ul-Husna Experience)

A spiritually sublime, visually breathtaking web application designed to foster deep contemplation, understanding, and memorization of the Divine Names of Allah (**Asma-ul-Husna**). 

The platform synthesizes classical theological precision and Quranic linguistic reflections, paired with curated nature visuals, micro-animations, and interactive memorization tools.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. The Name Allah & Traditional List of 99 Names
- **The Proper Name of Allah**: Features **Allah (اللَّٰه)** in a dedicated, unnumbered introductory card grounded in Surah Ta-Ha 20:8, followed by the traditional numbered list of 99 Names.
- **Academic & Evidence Categorization**: Every Name explicitly displays its evidence classification:
  - *Explicit in the Qur'an*
  - *Explicit in authentic Sunnah*
  - *Traditional-list formulation requiring further scholarly review* (marking the 25 specific names requiring review).
- **Understanding Iḥṣāʾ**: Features an "About This Collection" notice explaining the hadith of 99 Names (*Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7392* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2677a*) and clarifying that *iḥṣāʾ* (أَحْصَاهَا) encompasses preservation, deep comprehension, belief, calling upon Allah, and letting divine attributes shape one's conduct.
- **Large Quranic Arabic Typography**: Rendered with authentic **King Fahad Complex Othmani Madinah Quran Fonts** (`KFGQPC Uthmanic Script HAFS` and `KFGQPC Uthman Taha Naskh`) with full vocalization, tashkeel, diacritics, and interactive font-scaling controls (Standard, Large, Extra Large).
- **Comprehensive Scholarly Synthesis**:
  - **Meaning in Revelation**: Classical theological synthesis (drawing from Imam Al-Ghazali's *Al-Maqsad Al-Asna*, Ibn Al-Qayyim, and Ibn Kathir), divine attributes, and essential distinctions between similar names.
  - **Language & Qur’anic Context**: Arabic root morphology, semantic nuance, and Qur'anic context without speculative idioms.
  - **Evidence**: Key Qur'anic verses or authentic hadith citations.
  - **How the Believer Responds**: Practical character embodiment and spiritual practices in daily life.
  - **Supplications**: Strictly distinguishes between authentic supplications from the Qur'an/Sunnah and suggested personal du'as adhering to Arabic vocative rules.

### 2. Natural Imagery (Strictly No Faces or Human Figures)
- 100% pure natural landscapes, starry night skies, mountain peaks, crystal rivers, aurora borealis, and golden sand dunes from Unsplash that mirror the majesty of the Creator (*Khaliq*).

### 3. Interactive Learning & Quiz Studio
- **3D Interactive Flashcards**: Realistic 3D card-flip animation, shuffle deck, "Arabic First" or "Meaning First" toggle, and "Mark as Learned" tracking.
- **Names Quiz Companion**: Multiple choice quiz with 10-second timer, running streaks, scores, instant feedback, and celebratory particle confetti.
- **Learning Progress Tracking**: Real-time circular progress ring tracking learning of all 99 names (`X / 99`), saved persistently to `localStorage`.

### 4. Situational Du'a Explorer ("Call Upon Him")
- Explore Divine Names connected to emotional and life states:
  - *Anxiety, Stress & Restlessness*
  - *Broken Heart, Grief & Sadness*
  - *Guilt & Seeking Forgiveness*
  - *Financial Need & Career*
  - *Confusion & Seeking Guidance*
  - *Facing Injustice, Slander or Tyranny*
  - *Gratitude, Joy & Wonder*
