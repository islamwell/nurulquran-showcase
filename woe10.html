NURULQURAN PORTAL

Chapter 77 · The Ones Sent Forth

وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ

Woe, that Day, to the deniers.

Nasser Al-Qatami 77:15

SCROLL

Three words

One verdict.

وَيْلٌ

Woe

Arabic’s word for the worst. Extreme grief and extreme anger in one cry. It’s what you say when things can’t get any worse. Some narrations describe it as a valley in Hell.

يَوْمَئِذٍ

That Day

One word holds a whole scene. Its ending stands in for a full sentence: the Day when everything just described happens.

لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ

To the deniers

لِـ means “for”, so this woe has an address. It isn’t for people who never heard. It’s for people who heard, saw, and kept calling it a lie.

Ten echoes

Same words.
Heavier every time.

Each one comes right after a new proof. Watch the ring fill.

Beneath the surface

What the words hide.

It strikes first

Arabic sentences usually start with who or what. This one starts with the woe. The shock comes first and the reasons come later.

No size given

وَيْلٌ has no “the”. It’s just a woe, unnamed and unmeasured. Leaving it open makes it heavier.

Prayer and fact

It’s built like “peace be upon you”: part wish, part statement. Here it reads like a sentence already passed.

Same wind, two fates

The chapter opens by swearing by the winds. Let loose, they wreck. Held back, they bring rain. Revelation works the same way: mercy for whoever accepts it, ruin for whoever rejects it.

Ten times, never the same

Each echo follows a new proof. The words stay the same, but the weight grows, like a verdict read after every piece of evidence.

Not confusion. Choice.

They were shown the sky, history, birth and the earth. Nothing was missing. Denial here isn’t confusion. It’s a decision.

77 : 50

فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثٍ بَعْدَهُ يُؤْمِنُونَ

After this, what will they ever believe?

Ten proofs. Nothing left unexplained.
Now the question is for you.

Today, a warning.
That Day, a verdict.
You still have today.
