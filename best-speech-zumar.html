The Best Speech

Allah has sent down the best speech: a Book perfectly consistent, its lessons in pairs, returning again and again. The skin of those in awe of their Lord shivers from it — then their skins and hearts soften toward the remembrance of Allah. That is Allah's guidance; He guides with it whom He wills. And whoever Allah leaves astray has no guide.

One fabric, folded in pairs

Touch any star. Every part answers the others. Each piece of this Book matches the rest in beauty and truth. There's no weak page, and one verse explains another.

مَّثَانِيَ

Tap a card to fold it. The root means to fold, to double. Warnings come with good news, fear with hope. Its lessons return again and again, each time from a fresh angle.

Watch the order

  1. Skin first

    The words reach the body before the mind catches up. That's why even people who don't know Arabic have felt a shiver hearing it.

  2. ثُمَّ then…

    A pause. Awe is the doorway, not the destination. The verse never leaves you stuck in fear.

  3. Skins and hearts

    The softening goes deeper than the shiver ever did. It reaches the heart and turns it toward Allah.

That is guidance

If a verse ever made your skin shiver and then your heart grow soft, that wasn't random. That moment was guidance, a gift from Allah placed in your hands.

Guard it. Hearts that drift from His remembrance slowly harden again. And whoever Allah leaves astray has no one else to guide him.

Come back to it. It was made to be returned to — مَثَانِيَ.
