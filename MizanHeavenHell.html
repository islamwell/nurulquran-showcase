ACT III

دَارُ ٱلسَّلَام

Dār as-Salām — the Abode of Peace

i · Two Gardens

وَلِمَنْ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ جَنَّتَانِ

wa liman khāfa maqāma rabbihī jannatān

“And for the one who feared the standing before his Lord — two gardens.”

AR-RAḤMĀN 55:46

ii · No Harsh Weather

لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًا

lā yarawna fīhā shamsan wa lā zamharīrā

“They will find therein neither scorching sun nor bitter cold.”

AL-INSĀN 76:13

iii · Only Peace

لَا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا تَأْثِيمًا ۝ إِلَّا قِيلًا سَلَٰمًا سَلَٰمًا

illā qīlan salāman salāmā

“They hear no idle talk there, nor any sinful speech — only the saying: peace, peace.”

AL-WĀQIʿAH 56:25–26

iv · The Welcome

سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيْكُمْ طِبْتُمْ فَٱدْخُلُوهَا خَٰلِدِينَ

salāmun ʿalaykum ṭibtum fa-dkhulūhā khālidīn

“Peace be upon you; you have become pure — so enter it, to remain forever.”

AZ-ZUMAR 39:73

ACT IV

دَارُ ٱلْبَوَار

Dār al-Bawār — the Abode of Ruin

i · Its Fuel

ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِى وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلْحِجَارَةُ

an-nāra allatī waqūduhā an-nāsu wa-l-ḥijārah

“The Fire whose fuel is people and stones.”

AL-BAQARAH 2:24

ii · It Reaches the Heart

نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ ۝ ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ

nāru-llāhi al-mūqadah · allatī taṭṭaliʿu ʿalā-l-afʾidah

“The kindled Fire of Allah, which rises over the hearts.”

AL-HUMAZAH 104:6–7

iii · Lying in Wait

إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتْ مِرْصَادًا

inna jahannama kānat mirṣādā

“Indeed, Hell has ever been a place lying in wait.”

AN-NABAʾ 78:21

iv · No Rest

كُلَّمَا خَبَتْ زِدْنَٰهُمْ سَعِيرًا

kullamā khabat zidnāhum saʿīrā

“Every time it subsides, We increase for them the blaze.”

AL-ISRĀʾ 17:97

ACT V

ٱلْمِيزَان

Al-Mīzān — the Scale

A reflective object, not a calculator. Add to either side and watch the beam answer.

٠
Sayyi’āt
٠
Ḥasanāt

The beam rests level. Nothing has been placed.

This is a contemplative interaction — a piece of interface poetry. Deeds are not counted by any app, and the true weighing belongs to Allah alone. Treat it as an invitation to reflect, never as a verdict.

And yet — the door stays open

لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ ٱللَّهِ

“Do not despair of the mercy of Allah.”

AZ-ZUMAR 39:53

وَرَحْمَتِى وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ

“My mercy encompasses all things.”

AL-AʿRĀF 7:156
