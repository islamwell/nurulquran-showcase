Four pieces, one borrowed light

وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰ‍هَا
وَAnd by…

Right after swearing by the sun, Allah calls a second witness forward — not a rival to the sun, but her closest follower.

ٱلْقَمَرِthe moon

Cold rock hanging in blackness. It creates zero fire of its own. Notice it does not get a second oath for its brightness — because the brightness is not its own.

إِذَاwhen…

A condition. Not at all times — only when it stays lined up toward her. Drift out of position, and darkness takes back the surface.

تَلَىٰهَاit follows her step by step

Used in Arabic for a young one walking directly in its mother’s tracks — and the exact same Arabic word used for reciting the Qur’an faithfully, word after word.

Not a drop of light is its own

The sun owns two witnesses in the opening verse: itself and its own morning light. The moon owns neither. Turn it toward the unseen sun and its dark surface floods with silver. Turn it away, and all that remains is shadow.

Walking in her tracks

The Arabic word تَلَىٰ describes walking closely behind someone without overtaking or drifting away — and it is the very word for reciting revelation. True recitation is not noise on the tongue; it is keeping your face turned toward Allah’s light.

AlignedReceiving & reflecting light into the night.

إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا

When night blankets the sky, the sun has not vanished — it is simply out of view. The moon takes over the night shift, softening that blinding fire into peaceful silver so travelers can still see the road home.

One light, two roles

Every believer is asked to act like the moon: you never invent the light — you receive revelation, stay aligned with it, and softly shine it into dark places. Tap a card to shift the sky.

وَٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا

91:1 · The Source

Two oaths: the sun and its own radiant morning. Self-luminous, filling every valley. Like revelation itself — clear, warm, and needing nothing added to it.

وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا

91:2 · The Follower

One oath tied to a condition: when it follows her. Borrowed glow, gentle on the eyes, faithful even when darkness settles over the earth.
