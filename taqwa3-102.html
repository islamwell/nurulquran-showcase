01 — Depth

As He should be feared.

حَقَّ تُقَاتِهِۦḥaqqa tuqātihi

Not the panic of the shallows — the gravity of the deep. Awe is what the ocean teaches: that something can be measureless and still hold you.

Root
و ق ي
Literal
To shield
Occurrences
250+
02 — Ascent

The shield is worn inward.

Taqwā is the ultimate sanctuary and garment of the soul: وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيْرٌ. Wiqāyah: sacred protection from whatever distances the heart from Allah. Carried quietly, like snow held on stone.

03 — The Verse

“O you who have believed, fear Allah as He should be feared and do not die except as Muslims.”

Sūrah Āl ʿImrān · 3 : 102
04 — Horizon

Let the last light find you ready.

Every horizon is a reminder: the day closes on whatever state we kept.

05 — Taqwā in the Qur'ān · الآيات في التقوى

The Shield Defined In Divine Speech.

Across the Qur'ān, Allah illuminates Taqwā as spiritual garment, essential provision, an unfailing compass, and the doorway to divine companionship.

The Best Provision · خير الزاد
وَتَزَوَّدُوا۟ فَإِنَّ خَيْرَ ٱلزَّادِ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ۚ وَٱتَّقُونِ يَٰٓأُوْلِى ٱلْأَلْبَٰبِ

"And take provisions, but indeed, the best provision is Taqwā (God-consciousness). And fear Me, O you of understanding."

Sūrah Al-Baqarah · 2:197 سورة البقرة
The Best Garment · لباس التقوى
وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيْرٌ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ مِنْ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

"And the clothing of Taqwā — that is best. That is from the signs of Allah that perhaps they may remember."

Sūrah Al-Aʿrāf · 7:26 سورة الأعراف
The Divine Measure · أكرمكم عند الله
إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتْقَىٰكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرٌ

"Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most conscious of Him. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted."

Sūrah Al-Ḥujurāt · 49:13 سورة الحجرات
A Way Out & Ease · المخرج واليسر
وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُۥ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ

"And whoever fears Allah — He will make for him a way out, and will provide for him from where he does not expect."

Sūrah Aṭ-Ṭalāq · 65:2–3 سورة الطلاق
Criterion & Forgiveness · الفرقان
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِن تَتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّكُمْ فُرْقَانًا وَيُكَفِّرْ عَنكُمْ سَيِّـَٔاتِكُمْ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ

"O you who have believed, if you fear Allah, He will grant you a criterion [furqān] and will remove for you your misdeeds and forgive you."

Sūrah Al-Anfāl · 8:29 سورة الأنفال
Divine Companionship · معية الله
إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوا۟ وَّٱلَّذِينَ هُم مُّحْسِنُونَ

"Indeed, Allah is with those who fear Him and those who are doers of good."

Sūrah An-Naḥl · 16:128 سورة النحل
