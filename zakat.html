Quranic Categories · 9:60

The Eight Beneficiaries.
Who is Eligible for Zakat.

Allah Himself specified the eight groups eligible to receive Zakat in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60). Zakat must be given directly to these exact categories.

Surah At-Tawbah · 9:60
۞ إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلْفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلْمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلْعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا وَٱلْمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَفِى ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلْغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبْنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ ۖ فَرِيضَةً مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ
“Zakat is only for the poor, the needy, those who collect it, bringing hearts together, freeing captives, those in debt, in the cause of Allah, and the stranded traveler. This is an obligation from Allah. And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.” Surah At-Tawbah · 9:60
الفُقَرَاء

Al-Fuqarā'

The Extremely Poor

People who have virtually no income or belongings, and cannot afford basic necessities like food, clean water, or safe shelter.

Real Impact: Families with zero assets, refugees facing severe hunger.
المَسَاكِين

Al-Masākīn

The Working Poor

People who earn a living, but their income is not enough to cover basic daily living expenses. They do not beg out of dignity.

Real Impact: Low-income workers struggling to pay rent or essential heating.
العَامِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا

Al-'Āmilīna 'Alayhā

Zakat Administrators

Trustworthy workers hired by legitimate charities to collect, account for, safeguard, and deliver Zakat to verified recipients.

Real Impact: Caseworkers, verification officers, and field distribution teams.
المُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ

Al-Mu'allafah

New Muslims & Friends

New Muslims who lost family or financial support after accepting Islam, or individuals whose hearts need encouragement and care.

Real Impact: Supporting vulnerable converts and building community ties.
فِي الرِّقَاب

Fī Ar-Riqāb

Freeing Captives

Historically used to free slaves. Today, scholars apply this to rescue victims of human trafficking and modern forced labor.

Real Impact: Legal rescue of bonded laborers and human trafficking victims.
الغَارِمِين

Al-Ghārimīn

People in Debt

People overwhelmed by unavoidable debt from basic survival needs, emergency medical care, or natural disasters, with no way to repay.

Real Impact: Emergency hospital bills, disaster victims who lost their homes.
فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ

Fī Sabīlillāh

In the Cause of Allah

Activities dedicated to sharing the message of Islam, teaching the faith, protecting the oppressed, and serving community welfare.

Real Impact: Authentic Islamic education, dawah projects, and humanitarian relief.
ابْنِ السَّبِيل

Ibn As-Sabīl

The Stranded Traveler

Travelers or refugees who are cut off from their funds while away from home, even if they have money in their homeland, until they can get home.

Real Impact: Displaced asylum seekers, students stranded abroad in emergencies.
Motivation from the Quran

What Allah Promises to Those Who Give.
Never Fear That Charity Makes You Poor.

Throughout the Quran, Allah reminds us that giving for His sake is the greatest investment a human can make. Listen to and reflect on these six clear promises:

Promise 01

Allah Multiplies What You Give 700 Times and More

مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنۢبَتَتْ سَبْعَ سَنَابِلَ فِى كُلِّ سُنبُلَةٍ مِّا۟ئَةُ حَبَّةٍ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُضَٰعِفُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ

“The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is like a grain of wheat that grows seven ears, with a hundred grains in every ear. And Allah multiplies for whom He wills.”

Surah Al-Baqarah · 2:261
Key Motivation: A single dollar given with sincere intention is multiplied into hundreds of rewards in Allah's sight.
Promise 02

You Never Lose: Allah Guarantees He Will Replace It

وَمَا أَنفَقْتُم مِّن شَيْءٍ فَهُوَ يُخْلِفُهُ ۖ وَهُوَ خَيْرُ الرَّازِقِينَ

“Whatever you spend of anything in charity, He will replace it. And He is the best of providers.”

Surah Saba · 34:39
Key Motivation: You cannot become poor by giving for Allah's sake. He promises to replace whatever you give with something greater.
Promise 03

Giving Charity Brings Total Peace of Mind

الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُم بِاللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ سِرًّا وَعَلَانِيَةً فَلَهُمْ أَجْرُهُمْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ وَلَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ

“Those who spend their wealth night and day, secretly and openly, will have their reward with their Lord. They will have no fear, nor will they grieve.”

Surah Al-Baqarah · 2:274
Key Motivation: Charity cures anxiety. Allah takes away your fear of the future and grief over the past, giving you true peace in your heart.
Promise 04

A Goodly Loan to Allah with a Noble Reward

إِنَّ الْمُصَّدِّقِينَ وَالْمُصَّدِّقَاتِ وَأَقْرَضُوا اللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا يُضَاعَفُ لَهُمْ وَلَهُمْ أَجْرٌ كَرِيمٌ

“Indeed, the men and women who give charity and lend to Allah a good loan — it will be multiplied for them, and they will have an honorable reward.”

Surah Al-Hadid · 57:18
Key Motivation: Allah owns everything, yet He calls your charity a loan to Him, which He will repay with high honor in Jannah.
Promise 05

Fear of Poverty is from Satan — Allah Promises Abundance

الشَّيْطَانُ يَعِدُكُمُ الْفَقْرَ وَيَأْمُرُكُم بِالْفَحْشَاءِ ۖ وَاللَّهُ يَعِدُكُم مَّغْفِرَةً مِّنْهُ وَفَضْلًا ۗ وَاللَّهُ وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ

“Satan threatens you with poverty and orders you to be greedy. But Allah promises you forgiveness from Him and abundant bounty. And Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing.”

Surah Al-Baqarah · 2:268
Key Motivation: When you hesitate to give, know that the fear of running out of money is a whisper from Satan. Allah's promise is forgiveness and abundance.
Promise 06

Give Today Before Death Comes

وَأَنفِقُوا مِن مَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم مِّن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوْلَا أَخَّرْتَنِي إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ قَرِيبٍ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ الصَّالِحِينَ

“And spend from what We have provided you before death comes to one of you and he says: ‘My Lord, if only You would delay me for a little while so I could give charity and be among the righteous!’”

Surah Al-Munafiqun · 63:10
Key Motivation: In the final moments of life, people do not ask for more time to enjoy wealth — their only wish is to return to life to give charity. Give while you have the chance.

Small & Consistent Giving

The Prophet ﷺ said:
“Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date.”

Nothing is too small in the sight of Allah. Giving even a small amount regularly with a sincere heart protects you from hardship and brings blessings into your home.
