Quranic Jurisprudence · 9:60

The Eight Beneficiaries.
Divinely Chosen Recipients.

Allah Himself designated the exact categories eligible to receive Zakat in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60). No human, caliph, or scholar has authority to invent a ninth category.

Surah At-Tawbah · 9:60
۞ إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلْفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلْمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلْعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا وَٱلْمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَفِى ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلْغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبْنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ ۖ فَرِيضَةً مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ
“Zakat expenditures are only for the poor and for the needy and for those employed to collect [zakat] and for bringing hearts together [for Islam] and for freeing captives [or slaves] and for those in debt and for the cause of Allah and for the [stranded] traveler - an obligation [imposed] by Allah. الفُقَرَاء

Al-Fuqarā'

The Destitute

Those who possess virtually nothing — no income, no assets, or less than half of what is necessary to meet life's basic human requirements for food, water, and shelter.

Modern Impact: Displaced families with zero assets, refugees in famine conditions.
02 / 08
المَسَاكِين

Al-Masākīn

The Working Needy

Those who work or earn income, yet whose earnings fall short of fulfilling their essential monthly needs. They maintain dignity and do not beg, yet suffer in silence.

Modern Impact: Underpaid workers unable to afford rent, healthcare, or winter utilities.
03 / 08
العَامِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا

Al-'Āmilīna 'Alayhā

Zakat Administrators

Those appointed or employed by legitimate authorities to collect, account for, safeguard, and faithfully distribute Zakat to verified beneficiaries.

Modern Impact: Field workers, auditable caseworkers, and distribution logistics staff.
04 / 08
المُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ

Al-Mu'allafah

Reconciling Hearts

Individuals whose hearts are drawn to Islam, new converts who have lost family and financial support, or those whose harm against the community is averted through goodwill.

Modern Impact: Relieving ostracized converts, social integration, and bridge-building.
05 / 08
فِي الرِّقَاب

Fī Ar-Riqāb

Emancipating Bondage

Historically used to purchase freedom for enslaved individuals. In contemporary jurisprudence, applied to ransom unjust captives and rescue victims of human trafficking.

Modern Impact: Anti-trafficking legal rescues, bonded brick-kiln laborers, prison bond aid.
06 / 08
الغَارِمِين

Al-Ghārimīn

Debt-Stricken Souls

Those overwhelmed by inescapable debts incurred for permissible and necessary causes (e.g. emergency surgery, natural disaster loss, basic sustenance) without means to pay.

Modern Impact: Catastrophic hospital bills, earthquake victims who lost homes to debt.
07 / 08
فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ

Fī Sabīlillāh

In the Cause of Allah

Activities dedicated to defending the oppressed Muslim community, spreading the truth of Islam, public religious education, and establishing righteous community welfare.

Modern Impact: Authentic Islamic scholarship, dawah institutes, and defending persecuted communities.
08 / 08
ابْنِ السَّبِيل

Ibn As-Sabīl

The Wayfarer

The stranded traveler who is cut off from their wealth while away from home, even if they are wealthy in their homeland, until they can safely return.

Modern Impact: Displaced asylum seekers, stranded students abroad whose funding collapsed.

Clear Jurisprudence

Zakat vs. Sadaqah Jariyah.
Know the Difference.

Both purify the soul, but their legal rulings, eligible beneficiaries, and calculation mechanics operate under distinct divine guidelines.

Criterion Zakat al-Māl (الزَّكَاة) Sadaqah Jāriyah (صَدَقَة جَارِيَة)
Islamic Ruling Farḍ 'Ayn (Obligatory) — One of the 5 Pillars of Islam. Mustaḥabb (Voluntary / Sunnah) — Highly rewarded continuous charity.
Required Amount Fixed strictly at 2.5% (1/40th) of qualifying surplus wealth. Any amount — from half a date, a smile, a borehole, to a grand university.
Eligible Recipients Restricted strictly to the 8 Quranic categories in Surah 9:60. Open to all humans & creations — public utilities, relatives, non-Muslims, animals.
Threshold (Niṣāb) Must exceed 85g gold or 595g silver net wealth. No minimum threshold — even someone receiving Zakat can give Sadaqah.
Time Horizon Calculated after holding surplus for one lunar year (Ḥawl). Immediate; continuous rewards flow into the grave long after death.
